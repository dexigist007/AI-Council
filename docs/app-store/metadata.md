# App Store metadata (draft)

Keep "make money fast" wording out of the listing (guideline 2.3.1); describe what the app does.

## English (primary)

**Name (≤30):** pick a unique one, e.g. `Council — AI Debate`
**Subtitle (≤30):** `Several AIs weigh your ideas`

**Promotional text (≤170):**
Ask a panel of AI models instead of one. They analyse independently, challenge each other's claims,
and a judge writes a clear, realistic report.

**Description:**
AI Council puts several AI models around one table. Instead of trusting a single answer, you watch
them analyse your question independently, challenge each other's claims, and hand the final word to
a judge that weighs the arguments.

• Discovery — describe your goal, skills, budget and time; the council proposes options, critiques
  them and ranks a shortlist with revenue scenarios, a 7-day validation plan and clear stop criteria.
• Idea review — get a decision (build, validate, hold or reject) with a rubric, risks and an MVP scope.
• Live debate — follow every member's reasoning as it is written, colour-coded by seat.
• Project analysis — index a code folder, ask questions with verified citations, and run an audit.
• Decision log and idea graveyard — keep a record of what you decided and why.

Private by design: no account, no server, no analytics. You use your own API keys (Google Gemini,
OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, Cerebras and more) or free local models with Ollama
or LM Studio. Keys stay in your Keychain, and you approve each provider before any data is sent.

Reports are AI-generated suggestions, not financial, legal or tax advice.

**Keywords (≤100):** `AI,council,debate,LLM,ideas,startup,validation,Gemini,OpenAI,Claude,Ollama,code review`

## App Privacy answer
**Data Not Collected.** Apple defines "collect" as transmitting data off the device so that *you or your
third-party partners* can access it. The developer runs no server and embeds no third-party SDKs;
requests go straight from the device to an AI provider the user selects and pays for with their own key.
If you prefer the cautious route instead, declare *User Content → Other User Content* and *Other Data*,
purpose *App Functionality*, not linked to identity, not used for tracking. Whichever you choose, keep the
privacy policy consistent with it.
