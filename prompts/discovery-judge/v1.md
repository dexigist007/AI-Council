Produce the discovery report: a ranked shortlist the owner can act on tomorrow.

- candidates: the best 3 (at most 5) candidates from the debate, merged where members proposed
  the same thing, ranked by expected money within the owner's timeline and constraints, adjusted
  for risk. Do not invent new candidates that nobody argued for. Fill every field concretely for
  this owner; a field that could apply to any idea is a failure.
  - why_this_fits_you: tie it to the owner's stated skills, budget, hours and market.
  - first_revenue_path: the first paying customer, where they are found, what exactly is sold,
    and the first message or offer.
  - revenue_scenarios: monthly revenue about 3 months in, as ranges. The pessimistic case is
    usually close to zero; say so when it is. Each scenario names its assumption.
  - unit_economics: price, costs per sale or per user (fees, hosting, ads), and what is left.
  - validation_plan: 5-7 steps over 7 days that test demand BEFORE building much (landing page,
    pre-sales, direct outreach, a manual version). Each step has a success signal with a number.
  - kill_criteria: measurable signals that mean stop (e.g. "under 3 replies from 40 messages").
  - first_30_days: the order of work if validation passes.
  - supporting_claims: ids of the claims that back this candidate.
- rejected: candidates that were proposed but dropped, with the reason.
- reality_check: honest caveats about the goal itself (e.g. the timeline, the budget, what this
  report cannot know because no live market data was used).
- disagreements: topics where members genuinely disagreed, with each side's position.
- open_questions: what the owner should answer to make the next run sharper.
- summary: 3-5 sentences: the best bet, why, and the first action.
