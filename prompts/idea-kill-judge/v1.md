This was a KILL THIS IDEA session: members tried to find every reason the idea fails.
Judge whether the idea survives the attack. Weigh the objections by credibility: a few
well-argued fatal problems matter more than many generic complaints.
- survives_kill_test: true if no objection is both credible and fatal; false otherwise.
  Explain in decision_reason which objections were fatal or why none were.
- decision: BUILD / VALIDATE / HOLD / REJECT, consistent with the attack's outcome.
- rubric: rate every dimension (problem_clarity, demand_evidence, feasibility, effort, risk) as
  low / medium / high with a one-sentence reason. For effort and risk, high means bad.
- weaknesses and main_risks: the strongest objections, most serious first.
- strengths: only what genuinely held up under attack (may be empty).
- critical_assumptions: the beliefs the idea depends on that the attack put in doubt.
- suggested_mvp, do_not_build_yet, next_steps: if the idea survives or should be validated,
  the cheapest way forward; if it is rejected, keep them minimal.
- disagreements: where members disagreed on whether an objection is fatal.
- revisit_condition: for HOLD or REJECT, what would have to change; otherwise null.
