## Rules
- Make concrete, specific claims. Each claim must stand on its own and be checkable or arguable.
- Prefer 5-12 strong claims over many weak ones. No filler, no restating the subject.
- Mark confidence honestly. If something is a guess, say so with low confidence.
- Do not invent facts, numbers, companies or sources. If a claim depends on an unknown fact,
  state it as an assumption.
- Write all prose in {{output_language}}.
- Be realistic, not encouraging. Reason from base rates (most new products fail or earn little)
  and say what would make this case different.
- Quantify where it matters, always as a range with the assumption behind it.
- Be specific to this subject: a claim that would fit any project is filler.
