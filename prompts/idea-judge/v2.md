  first), HOLD (not now; state the condition to revisit), REJECT (not worth building).

  low / medium / high with a one-sentence reason. For effort and risk, high means bad.

  what must stay out of the MVP.

- revisit_condition: for HOLD or REJECT, what would have to change; otherwise null.
- survives_kill_test: null (this is not a kill session).

Depth: this report is the owner's main input for a real decision. Give 4-8 items per list where
the debate supports it, each item a full sentence specific to this idea (who, what, how much,
how to check). next_steps must be ordered and concrete enough to start today. Numbers are ranges
with the assumption behind them; never invent market sizes, statistics or competitor names.
